# Impact

The Impact tab is one story: food rescued from waste. Eating macros live on the separate **Eating** tab.

## One definition

**Rescued = used while inside your N-day window.**  
N is the expiry reminder in Food preferences (3, 5, 7, or 14 days). The same wording appears at the top of Impact and on the hero. The near-expiry flag is stored when you cook.

Items with no expiry date are never rescued. The page names how many meals used only undated food and were left out of the kilogram total.

## What the page shows

1. **Hero** — lifetime rescued kg (counts up after a cook), CO₂e as a WRAP-derived range, this week’s aim as a thin progress bar, dual 7-day bars (this week / last week, with legend), weekday labels, 0 on empty days, tap for kg, week-versus-last-week line, best milestone. Share photographs this card.
2. **Caught in time** — stacked bar of pantry *uses* (counts only here): sage = inside the window, sky = still had longer. No “still fresh %” label.
3. **Top rescued foods** — kg bars; tap filters meals; a removable chip shows the active filter.
4. **Milestones** — one chip row. Month grid and cumulative line sit behind **See history**.

## CO₂e

Shown as a range using 2.5–2.9 kg CO₂e per kg, derived from WRAP national household food-waste totals:

- *Household Food and Drink Waste in the UK 2022*: ~16 Mt CO₂e / ~6.0 Mt ≈ 2.7  
- *Household Food and Drink Waste in the UK 2021/22*: ~18 Mt CO₂e / ~6.4 Mt ≈ 2.8  

WRAP does not publish a single per-kg product factor for waste avoided. **How we calculate** cites those reports. The old 3.8 figure (food bought, WRAP/WWF 2011 §8.1) is not used as a waste-prevented claim.

## Demo and wow moment

- After marking a meal eaten with near-expiry food: dialog/snackbar plus Impact hero count-up and “You just kept X kg of Y out of the bin.”
- Settings → Kitchen → **Load demo week** seeds ~14 days of cooking and rescues.
- Empty Impact still shows the bars at rest: “Cook something from your pantry to start.”

## Eating tab

Calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre for every goal, with the interactive week explorer and energy split. Not part of the rescued-kg story.
